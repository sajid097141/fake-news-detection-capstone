About the model and its limits

What it is

A TF-IDF text representation (single words and word pairs) feeding a Logistic Regression classifier. It was trained on 63,676 cleaned articles from the WELFake dataset, where label 0 means Fake and 1 means Real.

How it was tested

On 12,736 held-out WELFake articles it reached 95.90% accuracy and a 95.87% macro F1-score. That is a score on the same dataset it learned from, not a measure of real-world accuracy.

Where it falls short

It cannot verify facts or sources, and it may partly learn how certain publishers write. It works on English text. Confidence is the model's own probability, not a guarantee.

VERITAS is a Class XII Artificial Intelligence capstone project. Predictions run locally on this machine and no text is sent to external services.

Not a fact-checking service. Results are machine-learning predictions of text patterns and can be wrong. Verify important claims with trusted sources.
